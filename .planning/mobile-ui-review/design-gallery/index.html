System

One quiet ground, white for the one primary action and selected states, colour only from artwork. Every board on this canvas is generated from these tokens.

Only Regular, Semibold and Bold ship in the app (500 renders as Regular), so the round-4 weight 500 is gone. Sentence case, no tracking, nothing under 12. Text scales with the system font size; rows grow instead of clipping.

Spacing, shape, targets

4812162432

Gutter 16. Radius: thumbnails 8, small controls and chips 8, large artwork, buttons and fields 12, sheets 16. Circles only for people (artists) and the play button; the only pill shapes are switch and slider tracks. Every touch target 48 × 48, even when the visible control is smaller.

One family: Ionicons, 24, weight 400. Outlined at rest; filled only for selected, active or media transport. Ionicons and this outlined/filled mapping are already shipped.

Motion (existing tokens in src/ui)

PressTactile spring, sink + dim, held 70 ms
Small stateMotion.duration.fast 160, decelerate
Sheet open / closeMotion.spring.open (critically damped); a release keeps finger speed
Player expandOne sheet from the mini player; open spring 400, fling 1500
List changeRows slide with transforms; 40 ms stagger, max 6
Reduce MotionEvery move becomes a fade; no feature removed

Transform and opacity only. No idle loops on browse screens; at most two self-running effects per screen (player glow + canvas).

Interaction rules

Row tap plays. ⋮ and long press open the same sheet; long press is a shortcut, never the only way.

Playback shows the player’s truth. A tap on Play shows pressed feedback, then a spinner until audio starts. The playing badge appears only when the player reports playing.

Sheets: one component; Back, a swipe down or the scrim closes and focus returns to the control that opened it.

Undo before confirm: reversible actions (remove from playlist) act at once with Undo; irreversible ones (delete song, delete playlist) ask first.

Sort: a labelled button opens a half-height sheet; the choice applies at once and is remembered per list.

Back: closes the top sheet, then the player, then pops the page, then leaves the tab for Stream.
