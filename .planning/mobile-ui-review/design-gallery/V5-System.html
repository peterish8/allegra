Interaction rules

Row tap plays. ⋮ and long press open the same sheet; long press is a shortcut, never the only way.

Playback shows the player’s truth. A tap on Play shows pressed feedback, then a spinner until audio starts. The playing badge appears only when the player reports playing.

Sheets: one component; Back, a swipe down or the scrim closes and focus returns to the control that opened it.

Undo before confirm: reversible actions (remove from playlist) act at once with Undo; irreversible ones (delete song, delete playlist) ask first.

Sort: a labelled button opens a half-height sheet; the choice applies at once and is remembered per list.

Back: closes the top sheet, then the player, then pops the page, then leaves the tab for Stream.
