# LuvLyrics redesign: "lyric-led, plain" (draft for owner review, 2026-11-05)

Status: mockups only. Nothing here is implemented until the owner says go. Mockups live on the "Redesign" page of
the design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/UGiYJ1UaRjWA66qTFjsmHT; the "Current app" page shows today's screens
for comparison.

## The idea

A standard music app layout (people know where things are) with one twist that is ours: **the lyric line is the
second voice of the whole app.** The pill shows the line being sung instead of the artist, Now playing shows a live
three-line lyric card under the transport, Luvs cards lead with the line. Everything else is quiet.

## What goes

- Every decorative gradient, colour wash, veil, vignette, grain, sheen and stacked glow. Grounds are flat.
- Idle loops: spinning disc, mascot, info-tour animations, drifting orbs, auto-advancing stories.
- The DJ mascot (DJ becomes a prompt + queue screen).
- The "i" tours and explanatory subtitles under headings.
- The Library cover deck (replaced by a plain "Jump back in" shelf and a list).
- The mic slot in the tab bar (voice search moves into the Search field).

## What stays

- Lime `#d9e66a` as the one accent: play buttons, progress, the active state. Coral `#ee6b5f` only for liked.
- Frosted glass only on the two floating things: the tab bar and the pill. Everything else is solid.
- The four tabs: Stream, Luvs, Library, More.
- All features. This is a look-and-layout change, not a feature cut.

Type (Roboto, weights 400/500/600/700 only, no letter spacing, no uppercase):
display 32/700 (page titles), title 22/600, lyricLarge 28/700, section 18/600, row 16/500, body 15/400,

Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 (screen gutter), 24, 32. Rows 64 pt. Touch targets 44 pt minimum.

## Screens

1. **Library**: big title + search/add; chips Songs, Playlists, Artists, Albums, Downloaded; "Jump back in" shelf;
   "Songs" header with count and sort; 64 pt rows (playing row: title in accent + bars).
2. **Playlists**: 2-column grid of square covers (2×2 mosaic for user playlists), name + count; first tile "New".
3. **Playlist**: centred 200 cover, title, meta, action row (Play pill, Shuffle, Download, •••), rows. Flat ground.
4. **Song actions sheet**: solid surface sheet, song head, icon rows, destructive group last.
5. **Stream**: title, search field, mood chips, Quick picks (2×4 list pager), Made for you shelf.
6. **Search**: field with mic, Recent list, Browse grid of flat tone tiles.
7. **Now playing**: flat ground tinted from the cover (solid), 320 art, title/artist + heart, scrubber, transport,
   live lyric card (prev / current / next line) that opens full lyrics; footer row Up next, device, lyrics.
8. **Lyrics fullscreen**: compact header (art 40, title, close), big lines with current bright, mini transport.
9. **Up next**: sheet with the playing song, then queue rows with drag handles; Shuffle, Clear.
10. **Luvs**: one card per screen: cover, the lyric line large, song/artist, actions column (Luv, Save, Full song).
11. **Settings**: grouped panels of rows (plain icon, label, value, chevron), Player look as a segmented control.
12. **Your DJ**: prompt field, suggestion chips, Now spinning card, Up next from the DJ. No mascot.
13. **Blends**: Create a Blend button, rows with two overlapping avatars, name, status line.
14. **Artist**: square photo block with the name UNDER it (no text over image, so no gradient needed), Shuffle /
    Follow, Top songs, Albums shelf.

## Floating chrome (identical on every tab screen)

- Tab bar: 12 pt from the sides and bottom, 64 tall, glass, radius 22. Icon 24 + label 11/500. Active: ink, filled
  glyph. Rest: ink3, outline glyph.
- Pill: 8 pt above the bar, 60 tall, glass, radius 18. 44 art (radius 8), title 14/600, current lyric line 12 ink2,
  next + play (36 accent circle). A 2 pt accent progress line along the bottom edge.

## Motion (unchanged rules, fewer moves)

transform/opacity only, tokens only, Reduce Motion → opacity. No idle loops. Press: 0.97 scale + surface2 fill.
