<objective>
Stop keeping removed songs and playlists forever. Rows for an unliked song, a deleted playlist
or a removed playlist item are kept as "tombstones" so offline devices learn about the removal.
Delete tombstones older than 90 days, and tell any device whose cursor predates the deletion to
resync from scratch.

Who is affected: the web always reads the feed from revision 0 (`fetchLibraryChanges` in
`apps/web/src/lib/api.ts`), so it needs no change. The phone stores a cursor and needs a resync
path.

This plan changes sync, the most failure-prone area in the app. It ends with a development-device
checkpoint; local tests can verify the protocol, but they do not replace a physical-device run.
</objective>
